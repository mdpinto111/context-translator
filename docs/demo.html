A Sunday by the river

By the editors · 4 min read

A quiet morning

The river was calm that morning. We sat on the bank and watched the boats drift by, waiting for the fish to bite. Later it started to rain, so we packed up and went home.

Money matters

I needed cash for the trip. The bank was closed on Sunday, so I used an ATM at the station instead. It charged a fee, but it saved the day.

Lessons learned

Check the weather, bring cash, and never underestimate how fast a river can rise after a storm.
